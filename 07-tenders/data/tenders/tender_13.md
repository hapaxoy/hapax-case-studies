# Invitation to Tender

**Buyer:** Jarviseudun hyvinvointialue
**Procurement:** Technical and property maintenance framework agreement — HVAC, electrical, and building automation services for health and social care premises
**Reference:** JHVA-2026-TEK-014
**Procedure:** Open procedure under the Act on Public Procurement and Concession Contracts; a notice corresponding to this invitation has been published through the wellbeing county's standard procurement channel
**Date of issue:** 2 October 2026

---

## 1. Background

Jarviseudun hyvinvointialue is responsible for health and social care premises across its area, including health stations, a district hospital wing, and associated support buildings. The technical building systems in these properties — heating, ventilation and air conditioning, electrical installations, lifts, fire protection, and building automation — require continuous, competently resourced maintenance to keep the buildings operational for patient care. The wellbeing county has previously procured this class of service under framework arrangements and is now retendering the maintenance framework as the prior arrangement approaches the end of its term.

This document sets out the requirements for tenderers wishing to be considered for the framework agreement. It should be read together with its four annexes, which form an integral part of the invitation to tender and carry the same binding weight as this document.

## 2. Scope of the procurement

The framework agreement covers scheduled and corrective maintenance of HVAC systems, electrical installations, lifts, fire protection systems, and building automation across the properties listed in Liite A, together with associated reporting, documentation, and continuity obligations. The framework period is intended to run for three years with two optional one-year extensions at the Buyer's sole discretion. Call-off orders will be issued against the framework as needs arise; the framework agreement itself does not guarantee any minimum order volume.

## 3. Eligibility requirements

A tenderer must meet all of the following requirements to be considered. Failure to meet any one of them will result in exclusion from the procurement, regardless of the technical or commercial merit of the tender submitted.

3.1 The Bidder shall hold a valid NMSB 7310 certificate.

3.2 The Bidder shall hold electrical contracting rights (class S2 or higher).

3.3 The Bidder shall hold a valid Urakkavarmenne Varmennettu Toimittaja ("Verified Supplier") certificate at the time the tender is submitted.

3.4 The Bidder's turnover for the most recently closed financial year shall be at least EUR 5,000,000.

3.5 The Bidder shall employ at least 20 HVAC (LVI) professionals in its own workforce.

3.6 The Bidder shall have at least one reference project with a value of at least EUR 300,000.

The Buyer reserves the right to verify any of the above by requesting supporting documentation before the framework agreement is signed.

## 4. Technical requirements

The tender shall address each of the following points. Responses should be specific to the properties and systems described in Liite A rather than generic statements of capability.

4.1 Content of the HVAC system maintenance agreement — the tenderer shall describe the scope, frequency, and reporting format of scheduled HVAC maintenance visits proposed for the contract period.

4.2 Handover of the asset register and documentation — the tenderer shall describe how it will receive, verify, and maintain the property asset register and associated technical documentation handed over by the Buyer at contract start.

4.3 Qualification requirements for personnel on site — the tenderer shall list the trade qualifications and site-access clearances its personnel will hold when working in occupied health and social care premises.

4.4 Regulatory compliance of lift maintenance — the tenderer shall describe how statutory lift inspection and maintenance obligations will be met and documented.

4.5 Occupational safety plan — the tenderer shall submit an occupational safety plan covering work in and around active patient-care areas.

4.6 Building automation system compatibility (BACnet/Modbus) — the tenderer shall confirm compatibility with the Buyer's existing building automation protocols and describe any integration work required.

4.7 Spare parts availability and delivery time — the tenderer shall state typical lead times for common spare parts and its approach to parts for discontinued equipment.

4.8 Quality control and testing procedures — the tenderer shall describe its internal quality control and testing procedures for completed maintenance and repair work.

4.9 Escalation procedure for unresolved faults — the tenderer shall describe the escalation path used when a reported fault is not resolved within the agreed response time.

4.10 Energy efficiency measures — the tenderer shall describe measures it will propose during the contract period to improve the energy efficiency of the maintained systems.

4.11 Fire protection system maintenance — the tenderer shall describe its approach to the scheduled testing and maintenance of fire protection systems in the properties.

4.12 Continuity of service through staff changes — the tenderer shall describe how continuity of service will be maintained if key maintenance personnel leave or are reassigned during the contract period.

4.13 Service level targets (SLA) — the tenderer shall propose service level targets for response and resolution times by fault category.

4.14 Periodic inspection of electrical installations — the tenderer shall describe its programme for statutory periodic inspection of electrical installations across the properties.

4.15 Commissioning and testing procedure — the tenderer shall describe the commissioning and testing procedure it will apply following any equipment replacement or major repair.

4.16 Maintenance of refrigeration equipment (F-gas) — the tenderer shall describe its approach to F-gas-regulated maintenance of refrigeration and cooling equipment on site.

## 5. Commercial terms

5.1 Limitation of liability clause — the tenderer shall state any limitation of liability it proposes to apply, with reference to the relevant clause of its standard terms if applicable.

5.2 Discount structure for framework agreement volume — the tenderer shall propose a discount structure applicable as call-off volumes under the framework increase over the contract period.

5.3 Pricing currency — all prices shall be quoted in euro.

5.4 Pricing structure (fixed price / unit price / time-based charging) — the tenderer shall set out its proposed pricing structure, distinguishing fixed-price maintenance visits, unit-priced parts and materials, and any time-based charging for call-out work.

5.5 Invoicing frequency and format — the tenderer shall state its proposed invoicing frequency and confirm its ability to invoice electronically in a format compatible with the Buyer's financial systems.

5.6 Payment terms — the tenderer shall confirm its acceptance of the payment terms set out in this invitation and its annexes.

5.7 Transparency of subcontractor costs — where subcontractors are used, the tenderer shall describe how subcontractor costs will be made transparent to the Buyer on request.

5.8 The payment term is 30 days net from the date of the invoice.

## 6. Forms and documents to be enclosed with the tender

6.1 Reference form (Liite 2) — to be completed and enclosed, listing the reference project(s) relied upon to satisfy Section 3.

6.2 Certificate of tax payment.

6.3 Description of the quality system.

6.4 CVs of key personnel proposed for the contract.

## 7. Format of the tender

7.1 Prices shall be quoted in EUR per square metre per year, not in EUR per month.

7.2 The tenderer shall state the delivery channel and the number of copies in which the tender is to be submitted, in accordance with the instructions given by the Buyer's procurement unit.

7.3 All monetary figures shall be rounded to two decimal places.

7.4 The minimum font size used in the tender document shall be 11 pt.

7.5 The tenderer shall note the time zone and exact closing time stated for the submission deadline in Section 9.

## 8. Evaluation of tenders

Tenders that pass the eligibility check in Section 3 and comply with the mandatory requirements of this invitation and its annexes will be compared on price (60%) and quality (40%). The quality comparison will be based on the responses given to Section 4 and Liite A, scored against the criteria published separately to tenderers who pass the eligibility stage. The Buyer reserves the right to reject all tenders without entering into a framework agreement if none is judged to represent acceptable value.

# Liite A — Tekninen erittely

This annex sets out the technical scope of the maintenance framework in more detail than the main body of the invitation. Where this annex and the main body both address a subject, the more detailed provision in this annex governs the technical scope of the work; commercial and eligibility matters remain as stated in the main body.

The properties covered are: Jarviseutu health station (main building and outpatient wing), Koivuranta health station, Mantyla health station, the district hospital's technical support wing, and the wellbeing county's administrative building. Combined gross floor area is approximately 28,000 m².

### 1. Subcontracting management plan

1.1 The tenderer shall describe how subcontracted trades will be selected, supervised, and quality-assured, including which portions of the work, if any, it intends to subcontract.

### 2. Method statement for installation works

2.1 The tenderer shall provide a method statement describing how installation and replacement works will be carried out in occupied buildings with minimal disruption to health and social care operations.

### 3. Maintenance planning

**3.1 Preventive maintenance plan**

The tenderer shall submit a preventive maintenance plan covering all system categories listed above, with proposed inspection intervals for each.

**3.2 Response time for fault situations and on-call arrangement**

The tenderer shall state its proposed response time for fault situations by severity category and describe the on-call arrangement it will maintain outside normal working hours, including how continuous cover is provided.

### 4. Warranty period for installed equipment

4.1 The tenderer shall state the warranty period it offers on equipment and materials installed under the framework agreement.

### 5. Training of the Buyer's property staff

5.1 The tenderer shall describe the training it will provide to the Buyer's own property staff on the operation of maintained systems, including building automation controls.

### 6. Inspection of the condition of the building envelope

6.1 The tenderer shall describe its proposed approach to periodic inspection of the condition of the building envelope (roofing, facades, and building fabric) as part of the wider maintenance scope.

### 7. General conditions applicable to this annex

The following general conditions apply to the technical scope described above, in addition to the individual points set out in Sections 1–6.

Maintenance visits shall be logged in the reporting system referred to in Section 4.7 of this annex on the day they are carried out. Materials used in maintenance and repair work shall meet the technical standards applicable to health and social care premises. Personnel working in patient-care areas shall observe the Buyer's infection-control and access instructions at all times.

7.1 If the Bidder cannot demonstrate round-the-clock on-call cover in Finland, the tender will be rejected regardless of price (absolute requirement, see Section 7.1 of this annex).

Any deviation from the scope described in this annex shall be agreed with the Buyer's technical contact in writing before work commences.

# Liite B — Kaupalliset ehdot

This annex sets out the commercial terms applicable to the framework agreement. It should be read together with Section 5 of the main body and Liite D (draft agreement).

### General pricing conditions

Prices submitted shall remain valid for 120 days from the closing date stated in Section 9 of the main body. The Buyer will not accept tenders that reserve the right to revise prices during this validity period except in accordance with the indexation clause below.

B.1 Bonus-malus scheme — the tenderer shall describe the bonus-malus arrangement it proposes for over- and under-performance against the agreed service level targets, including how any bonus or penalty amount would be calculated and applied.

B.2 Price indexation clause — the tenderer shall state the index it proposes to use for annual price adjustment during the framework period, and the reference month against which adjustments would be calculated.

### Payment and framework administration

B.3 Under the commercial terms of this annex, the payment term is set at 14 days net.

Invoices shall reference the relevant call-off order number and the property to which the work relates. Disputed invoice lines shall not delay payment of the undisputed portion.

B.4 Reference currency for framework agreement pricing — all framework pricing, including any indexed adjustments, shall be expressed and administered in euro.

The commercial terms in this annex apply for the duration of the framework period, including any extension exercised under Section 2 of the main body.

# Liite D — Sopimusluonnos

This draft agreement sets out the terms on which the Buyer intends to contract with the successful tenderer. It is provided for information at the tender stage; the final agreement signed with the successful tenderer will incorporate the tenderer's tender as an annex and may include minor drafting clarifications agreed during contract finalisation.

**1. Parties.** The agreement will be entered into between Jarviseudun hyvinvointialue ("the Buyer") and the successful tenderer ("the Contractor").

**2. Subject matter.** The Contractor undertakes to provide the maintenance services described in this invitation to tender and its annexes for the properties listed in Liite A.

**3. Term.** The agreement enters into force on the date stated in Section 9 of the main body and continues for the framework period stated there, subject to the Buyer's option to extend.

**4. Price and payment.** Pricing and payment terms are as set out in Liite B. In the event of any inconsistency between this draft agreement and Liite B, Liite B governs the commercial terms unless the final signed agreement expressly states otherwise.

**5. Personnel.** The Contractor shall ensure that personnel assigned to the contract meet the qualification requirements described in this invitation and remain suitably qualified throughout the contract period.

**6. Liability and insurance.** The Contractor shall maintain liability insurance appropriate to the scope of work for the duration of the agreement. Limitation of liability is as agreed under Section 5 of the main body.

**7. Termination.** Either party may terminate the agreement for material breach not remedied within 30 days of written notice. The Buyer may terminate for convenience on six months' written notice.

**8. Governing law.** The agreement is governed by Finnish law.

**9. Signatures.** [To be completed on execution of the agreement.]
