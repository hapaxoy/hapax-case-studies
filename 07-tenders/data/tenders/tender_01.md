# REQUEST FOR QUOTATION — Technical Property Maintenance Services

**Koskimyllyn Kiinteistot Oy**
Reference: KMK-2026-03
Issued: 10 March 2026
Contact: hankinnat@koskimyllynkiinteistot.example

## 1. Background

Koskimyllyn Kiinteistot Oy owns and manages a portfolio of commercial and light-industrial properties in the Jyvaskyla region. The current technical maintenance agreement for the portfolio expires on 31 May 2026, and the company is inviting quotations for a replacement service covering HVAC, electrical and building automation maintenance.

## 2. Scope

The successful tenderer will provide scheduled and corrective technical maintenance for the properties listed in Appendix 2 (property list, issued to shortlisted tenderers on request). Section 3 sets out the conditions a tenderer must meet to be considered; Sections 4 and 5 set out the technical and commercial content expected in the response.

## 3. Eligibility requirements

Both conditions below must be met. Supporting evidence should be submitted with the tender; certificates and financial statements may be requested for verification before contract award.

3.1 The tenderer must hold a valid LUSA qualification (LUSA-patevyys) for the relevant trade.

3.2 The tenderer's turnover must be at least EUR 5,000,000 in the most recently closed financial year.

## 4. Technical requirements

Tenderers should address each of the following in their technical proposal:

4.1 Handover of the asset register and associated documentation at the start of the contract, and the arrangements for keeping this documentation current thereafter.

4.2 Proposed service level targets (SLA) for response and completion of maintenance work, by priority category.

4.3 Method for inspecting the condition of the building envelope (roofing, facades, windows) as part of the annual maintenance cycle.

4.4 Commissioning and testing procedure to be followed after any equipment replacement or repair.

4.5 Warranty period offered on installed equipment and replacement parts.

## 5. Commercial terms

5.1 Proposed invoicing frequency and format.

5.2 Proposed contract period, including any extension options.

## 6. Tender submission

6.1 Tenderers must complete and return the Tender Form (Appendix 1) together with their technical and commercial proposal.

Pricing must observe the following conventions:

6.2 Price must be quoted in EUR/m²/year, not EUR/month.

6.3 Tenderers must state the unit basis applied to the maintenance pricing — per property or per square metre — and apply it consistently throughout the quotation.

Tenders must be submitted by e-mail to hankinnat@koskimyllynkiinteistot.example no later than **14 April 2026, 12:00 (Helsinki time)**. Tenders received after this time will not be considered.

## 7. Evaluation

Tenders will be evaluated on price (50%), proposed service levels and technical approach (35%), and references (15%). Koskimyllyn Kiinteistot Oy reserves the right to reject all tenders without giving reasons and to request clarifications from any tenderer during evaluation.

## Annex A — Tekniset ja kaupalliset liitteet (Technical and Commercial Appendices)

The following must be included as attachments to the tender:

A.1 Occupational safety plan (tyoturvallisuussuunnitelma) for work carried out on the properties.

A.2 Description of spare parts availability and delivery time for the equipment types covered by the contract.

A.3 CVs of the key personnel proposed for the contract.
