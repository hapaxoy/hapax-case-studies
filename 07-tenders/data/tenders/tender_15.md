# Invitation to Tender

**Buyer:** Suurjarven kaupunki
**Procurement:** Technical and property maintenance framework agreement — HVAC, electrical, and building automation services for municipal properties
**Reference:** SJK-2026-TEK-009
**Procedure:** Open procedure under the Act on Public Procurement and Concession Contracts; a notice corresponding to this invitation has been published through the city's standard procurement channel
**Date of issue:** 18 September 2026

---

## 1. Background

Suurjarven kaupunki owns and operates a range of municipal properties, including school buildings, a sports and leisure centre, and administrative premises, whose technical building systems require ongoing scheduled and corrective maintenance. The city is retendering its maintenance framework for these systems and invites tenders from suitably qualified contractors.

This document should be read together with its four annexes, which form an integral part of the invitation and carry the same binding weight as this document.

## 2. Scope of the procurement

The framework agreement covers scheduled and corrective maintenance of HVAC systems, electrical installations, lifts, fire protection systems, and building automation across the properties listed in Liite A, together with associated reporting and documentation obligations. The framework period is intended to run for four years, with the city retaining discretion to extend by a further year. Call-off orders will be issued against the framework as needs arise; the framework agreement itself does not guarantee any minimum order volume.

## 3. Eligibility requirements

A tenderer must meet all of the following requirements to be considered. Failure to meet any one of them will result in exclusion from the procurement, regardless of the technical or commercial merit of the tender submitted.

3.1 The Bidder shall have at least one reference project with a value of at least EUR 300,000.

3.2 The Bidder shall have at least three (3) comparable reference projects within the last five years.

3.3 The Bidder shall have at least one reference project with a value of at least EUR 500,000.

3.4 The Bidder shall employ at least 20 HVAC (LVI) professionals in its own workforce.

3.5 The Bidder shall hold a valid public liability insurance with cover of at least EUR 1,000,000.

3.6 The Bidder shall hold a valid Urakkavarmenne Varmennettu Toimittaja certificate at the time the tender is submitted.

3.7 The Bidder shall have at least one reference project with a contract value of at least EUR 2,000,000 within the last five years.

The Buyer reserves the right to verify any of the above by requesting supporting documentation before the framework agreement is signed.

## 4. Technical requirements

4.1 Regulatory compliance of lift maintenance — the tenderer shall describe how statutory lift inspection and maintenance obligations will be met and documented.

4.2 Access control and security at the Buyer's premises — the tenderer shall describe how site-access and security procedures will be observed in school and public-facing buildings.

4.3 Reporting practice to the Buyer — the tenderer shall describe how maintenance activity and outstanding items will be reported to the city's facilities contact.

4.4 Building automation system compatibility (BACnet/Modbus) — the tenderer shall confirm compatibility with the city's existing building automation protocols.

4.5 Training of the Buyer's property staff — the tenderer shall describe training it will provide to the city's own property staff on maintained systems.

4.6 Method statement for installation works — the tenderer shall provide a method statement for installation and replacement work in occupied buildings, including school premises during term time.

4.7 Quality control and testing procedures — the tenderer shall describe its internal quality control and testing procedures for completed work.

4.8 Service level targets (SLA) — the tenderer shall propose service level targets for response and resolution times by fault category.

4.9 Fire protection system maintenance — the tenderer shall describe its approach to scheduled testing and maintenance of fire protection systems.

4.10 Waste management and sorting of waste on site — the tenderer shall describe how waste generated during maintenance work will be managed and sorted in accordance with the city's waste requirements.

4.11 Warranty period for installed equipment — the tenderer shall state the warranty period offered on equipment and materials installed.

4.12 Response time for fault situations and on-call arrangement — the tenderer shall state its proposed response time by severity category and describe out-of-hours cover.

4.13 Condition-monitoring data collection — the tenderer shall describe how condition-monitoring data will be collected and reported.

4.14 Content of the HVAC system maintenance agreement — the tenderer shall describe the scope and frequency of scheduled HVAC maintenance proposed.

4.15 Environmental management during works — the tenderer shall describe how environmental impacts will be managed during maintenance and repair work.

## 5. Commercial terms

5.1 Pricing structure (fixed price / unit price / time-based charging) — the tenderer shall set out its proposed pricing structure.

5.2 Contractual penalty for shortfall in service level — the tenderer shall describe its position on a contractual penalty applying where agreed service levels are not met.

5.3 Pricing currency — all prices shall be quoted in euro.

5.4 Invoicing frequency and format — the tenderer shall state its proposed invoicing frequency and confirm electronic invoicing capability.

5.5 Bonus-malus scheme — the tenderer shall describe the bonus-malus arrangement it proposes for over- and under-performance against agreed service levels.

5.6 Transparency of subcontractor costs — where subcontractors are used, the tenderer shall describe how their costs will be made transparent to the Buyer on request.

5.7 Discount structure for framework agreement volume — the tenderer shall propose a discount structure applicable as call-off volumes increase.

## 7. Format of the tender

7.1 The tender shall be submitted as a single PDF file, without password protection.

7.2 All prices shall be quoted excluding VAT, in euro.

7.3 The minimum font size used in the tender document shall be 11 pt.

7.4 All monetary figures shall be rounded to two decimal places.

7.5 All framework agreement pricing shall be expressed in euro as the reference currency.

## 8. Evaluation of tenders

Tenders that pass the eligibility check in Section 3 and comply with the mandatory requirements of this invitation and its annexes will be compared on price (55%) and quality (45%). The quality comparison will be based on the responses given to Section 4 and Liite A, scored against criteria published separately to tenderers who pass the eligibility stage. The Buyer reserves the right to reject all tenders without entering into a framework agreement if none is judged to represent acceptable value.

# Liite A — Tekninen erittely

This annex sets out the technical scope in more detail than the main body. Where this annex and the main body both address a subject, the more detailed provision in this annex governs the technical scope of the work.

The properties covered are: Suurjarvi comprehensive school, Rantakoulu primary school, the municipal sports and leisure centre, and the city's administrative building on Kauppatori. Combined gross floor area is approximately 24,500 m².

A.1 Content and scheduling of maintenance visits — the tenderer shall state the proposed content and frequency of scheduled maintenance visits for each property category.

A.2 Commissioning and testing procedure — the tenderer shall describe the commissioning and testing procedure following equipment replacement.

A.3 Spare parts availability and delivery time — the tenderer shall state typical lead times for common spare parts and its approach for discontinued equipment.

A.4 Escalation procedure for unresolved faults — the tenderer shall describe the escalation path used when a reported fault is not resolved within the agreed response time.

A.5 Periodic inspection of electrical installations — the tenderer shall describe its programme for statutory periodic inspection of electrical installations across the properties.

A.6 Qualification requirements for personnel on site — the tenderer shall list the trade qualifications and site-access clearances its personnel will hold when working in school and public buildings.

A.7 Occupational safety plan — the tenderer shall submit an occupational safety plan covering work in and around occupied school and public buildings.

General note: maintenance visits to school buildings during term time shall be scheduled, where practicable, outside teaching hours; the on-call arrangement described under Section 4 applies to all properties in this annex equally.

# Liite D — Sopimusluonnos

This draft agreement sets out the terms on which the Buyer intends to contract with the successful tenderer. It is provided for information at the tender stage; the final agreement signed with the successful tenderer will incorporate the tenderer's tender as an annex.

**1. Parties.** The agreement will be entered into between Suurjarven kaupunki ("the Buyer") and the successful tenderer ("the Contractor").

**2. Subject matter.** The Contractor undertakes to provide the maintenance services described in this invitation to tender and its annexes for the properties listed in Liite A.

**3. Term.** The agreement enters into force on the date stated in Section 9 of the main body and continues for the framework period stated there, subject to the Buyer's option to extend.

**4. Price and payment.** Pricing and payment terms are as set out in Liite B. In the event of any inconsistency between this draft agreement and Liite B, Liite B governs the commercial terms unless the final signed agreement expressly states otherwise.

**5. Personnel.** The Contractor shall ensure that personnel assigned to the contract meet the qualification requirements described in this invitation and remain suitably qualified throughout the contract period.

**6. Liability and insurance.** The Contractor shall maintain liability insurance appropriate to the scope of work for the duration of the agreement.

**7. Termination.** Either party may terminate the agreement for material breach not remedied within 30 days of written notice. The Buyer may terminate for convenience on six months' written notice.

**8. Governing law.** The agreement is governed by Finnish law.

**9. Signatures.** [To be completed on execution of the agreement.]
