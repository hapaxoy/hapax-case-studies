# Request for Quotation

**Buyer:** Teollisuuskiinteisto Rautavaara Oy
**Subject:** Technical maintenance services — HVAC, electrical, and building systems for the Rautavaara industrial estate
**Reference:** TKR-RFQ-2026-07
**Date of issue:** 22 September 2026

---

## 1. Background and scope

Teollisuuskiinteisto Rautavaara Oy owns and manages an industrial estate comprising four multi-tenant production and warehouse buildings, let to a mix of manufacturing and logistics tenants. The company is inviting quotations for a maintenance agreement covering the HVAC, electrical, building automation, and life-safety systems shared across the estate's common infrastructure and base-build systems. This is a private-sector procurement and is not subject to public procurement rules; the process described below reflects the company's own purchasing practice.

Bidders should read this request together with its four annexes, which are issued alongside this document and form part of the request.

## 2. Eligibility requirements

2.1 The Bidder shall hold a valid LUSA qualification for the relevant trade.

2.2 The Bidder shall employ at least 20 HVAC (LVI) professionals in its own workforce.

2.3 The Bidder shall hold an F-gas regulation qualification where the work involves refrigeration or air-conditioning equipment.

2.4 The Bidder shall hold a valid Urakkavarmenne Varmennettu Toimittaja certificate at the time the quotation is submitted.

2.5 The Bidder shall have at least one reference project with a value of at least EUR 300,000.

2.6 The Bidder shall employ at least 15 electrical professionals in its own workforce.

Bidders not meeting all of the above will not be considered further.

## 3. Technical requirements

3.1 Occupational safety plan — describe the occupational safety plan covering maintenance work across the multi-tenant estate.

3.2 Warranty period for installed equipment — state the warranty period offered on equipment and materials installed.

3.3 Regulatory compliance of lift maintenance — describe how statutory lift inspection and maintenance obligations will be met and documented for the estate's goods and passenger lifts.

3.4 Waste management and sorting of waste on site — describe how waste generated during maintenance work will be sorted and managed.

3.5 Spare parts availability and delivery time — state typical lead times for common spare parts and the approach for discontinued equipment.

3.6 Quality control and testing procedures — describe internal quality control and testing procedures for completed work.

3.7 Environmental management during works — describe how environmental impacts will be managed during maintenance and repair work.

3.8 Inspection of the condition of the building envelope — describe how the condition of roofing, cladding, and building fabric across the four buildings will be assessed.

3.9 Content and scheduling of maintenance visits — state the proposed content and frequency of scheduled maintenance visits.

3.10 Access control and security at the Buyer's premises — describe how site-access and security procedures will be observed across the tenanted buildings.

3.11 Reporting practice to the Buyer — describe how maintenance activity and outstanding items will be reported to the Buyer's facilities contact.

3.12 Response time for fault situations and on-call arrangement — state the proposed response time by severity category and describe out-of-hours cover.

3.13 Handover of the asset register and documentation — describe how the existing asset register and technical documentation will be received and maintained.

3.14 Response times for corrective maintenance — state proposed response times for corrective (non-emergency) maintenance requests.

3.15 Training of the Buyer's property staff — describe training that will be provided to the Buyer's own facilities staff on maintained systems.

3.16 Subcontracting management plan — describe how any subcontracted trades will be selected and supervised.

3.17 Escalation procedure for unresolved faults — describe the escalation path for a fault not resolved within the agreed response time.

## 4. Commercial terms

4.1 Bonus-malus scheme — describe the bonus-malus arrangement proposed for over- and under-performance against agreed service levels.

4.2 Price indexation clause — state the index proposed for annual price adjustment and the reference month for calculating adjustments.

4.4 Invoicing frequency and format — state proposed invoicing frequency and confirm electronic invoicing capability.

4.5 Contract termination terms — set out the notice periods and grounds for termination the Bidder proposes.

4.6 Pricing structure (fixed price / unit price / time-based charging) — set out the proposed pricing structure.

# Liite A — Tekninen erittely

This annex sets out the technical scope in more detail. The estate comprises four buildings (Halli 1 through Halli 4), combined gross floor area approximately 19,800 m², each let to a separate industrial or logistics tenant under a triple-net lease that reserves base-build and shared-system maintenance to the Buyer.

A.1 Qualification requirements for personnel on site — list the trade qualifications and site-induction requirements personnel will hold when working across the tenanted buildings.

A.2 Method statement for installation works — provide a method statement for installation and replacement work that minimises disruption to tenants' operations.

A.3 Maintenance of refrigeration equipment (F-gas) — describe the approach to F-gas-regulated maintenance of any refrigeration or cooling equipment serving the shared systems.

A.4 Content of the HVAC system maintenance agreement — describe the scope and frequency of scheduled HVAC maintenance proposed for the shared systems.

A.5 Continuity of service through staff changes — describe how continuity of service will be maintained if key maintenance personnel leave or are reassigned.

A.6 Periodic inspection of electrical installations — describe the programme for statutory periodic inspection of electrical installations across the estate.

A.7 Fire protection system maintenance — describe the approach to scheduled testing and maintenance of fire protection systems across the four buildings.

General note: access to tenanted areas requires at least 48 hours' notice to the relevant tenant except in emergency response situations, which are governed by Section 3 above.

# Liite D — Sopimusluonnos

This draft sets out the terms on which Teollisuuskiinteisto Rautavaara Oy intends to contract with the successful bidder. It is issued for information alongside the request and will be finalised with the chosen bidder before signature.

**1. Parties.** The agreement will be between Teollisuuskiinteisto Rautavaara Oy ("the Buyer") and the successful bidder ("the Contractor").

**2. Subject matter and term.** The Contractor will provide the maintenance services described in this request and its annexes for the properties described in Liite A, from the contract start date stated in Section 7, for an initial period of three years.

**3. Price.** Pricing is as quoted by the Contractor and accepted by the Buyer, subject to the commercial terms in Liite B.

**4. Enclosures.** The Contractor's quotation, including all forms and attachments required by this request, is incorporated into and forms part of this agreement by reference.

**5. Personnel and access.** The Contractor shall ensure personnel meet the qualification and access requirements described in Liite A and observe the site rules of individual tenants where applicable.

**6. Liability.** Each party's liability is limited as set out in the Contractor's accepted commercial terms, save for liability that cannot be limited by law.

**7. Termination.** Either party may terminate for material breach not remedied within 30 days of written notice, and otherwise on the notice terms proposed under Section 4 and accepted by the Buyer.

**8. Governing law.** The agreement is governed by Finnish law.

**9. Signatures.** [To be completed on execution of the agreement.]
