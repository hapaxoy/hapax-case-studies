# Tender notice — Technical building systems maintenance and installation framework agreement

**Contracting authority:** Pohjois-Keitele hyvinvointialue, Toimitilapalvelut
**Reference:** PKHYVA-2026-0087
**Procurement procedure:** Open procedure (avoin menettely)
**Notice published:** 1 September 2026
**Tender deadline:** 13 October 2026, 12:00 (EET)
**Planned contract start:** 1 February 2027
**Contract period:** 5 years, with the option to extend described in Section 5

## 1. Contracting authority and background

Pohjois-Keitele hyvinvointialue (the "wellbeing services county") is responsible
for health and social-services properties across its area, including health
centres, care facilities and administrative buildings. The wellbeing services
county is procuring a framework agreement for technical building systems
maintenance together with associated installation work arising from planned
system upgrades over the contract period.

This notice and its two annexes together form the complete tender documentation.
A requirement stated in only one part of this notice is nonetheless binding in
full.

## 2. Scope of the procurement

The framework agreement covers maintenance of electrical installations,
elevators, fire-protection systems and building automation across the wellbeing
services county's health-service properties, together with installation work
under call-off orders where building systems are being upgraded or replaced.
Individual assignments will be issued against the framework agreement across the
contract period; the agreement carries no minimum purchase commitment.

## 3. Eligibility requirements

Tenderers must satisfy the following requirements to be considered eligible.

3.1 Tarjoajalla tulee olla voimassa oleva NMSB 7310 -sertifikaatti.

3.2 Tarjoajalla tulee olla sahkourakointioikeudet (vahintaan luokka S2).

3.3 Tarjoajalla tulee olla vahintaan yksi referenssikohde, jonka arvo on ollut vahintaan 500 000 euroa.

A tenderer that does not meet all three eligibility requirements will not have
its tender evaluated further.

## 8. Evaluation criteria and contract award

The framework agreement will be awarded to the economically most advantageous
tender on the following basis:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Price | 50% |
| Technical quality of the maintenance and installation plan | 35% |
| References | 15% |

The wellbeing services county reserves the right to request clarifications from
tenderers during evaluation without altering the substance of a tender.

## 9. Submission instructions

Tenders must be submitted in full accordance with the annexes to this notice by
the deadline given on the first page. Tenders received after the deadline will
not be opened. Questions concerning this notice may be submitted in writing up
to ten (10) days before the deadline; answers will be circulated to all
tenderers who have registered an interest. The wellbeing services county does
not reimburse any costs incurred in preparing a tender.
