# REQUEST FOR QUOTATION — Technical Facility Maintenance Services

**Rantatuote Logistiikka Oy**
Reference: RTL-2026-07
Issued: 11 May 2026

## 1. Background

Rantatuote Logistiikka Oy operates a network of logistics and warehouse properties in the Jyvaskyla region. The company is inviting quotations for technical facility maintenance services covering HVAC, electrical, building automation and lift maintenance.

## 2. Scope

The contract covers scheduled and corrective maintenance across the properties listed in Appendix 2. Tenderers are expected to propose a service model suited to a logistics operation running extended hours.

## 3. Eligibility requirements

3.1 The tenderer must hold valid public liability insurance with cover of at least EUR 1,000,000.

3.2 The tenderer's equity ratio must be at least 20%.

## 4. Technical requirements

4.1 Escalation procedure for unresolved faults.

4.2 Qualification requirements for personnel working on site.

4.3 Environmental management arrangements during the course of the work.

4.4 Compatibility of the building automation system (BACnet/Modbus).

## 5. Commercial terms

5.1 Pricing structure (fixed price / unit price / time and materials).

5.2 Invoicing frequency and format.

## 6. Submission requirements

6.1 Description of the tenderer's quality management system.

6.2 All prices must be quoted exclusive of VAT, in EUR.

Tenders must be submitted by e-mail to hankinta@rantatuotelogistiikka.example no later than **19 June 2026, 12:00 (Helsinki time)**.

## 7. Evaluation

Tenders will be evaluated on price (45%), technical approach (35%) and quality management (20%).
