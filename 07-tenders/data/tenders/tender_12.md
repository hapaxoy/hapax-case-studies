## 1. Contracting authority (Hankintayksikko)

Keski-Vaaran kaupunki is procuring, on behalf of its technical services department, a framework agreement for the technical maintenance of the city's school properties. The procurement is carried out in accordance with the principles of national public procurement legislation and the contracting authority's own procurement instructions. This is not a request submitted through any national tendering portal; tenderers are asked to follow the submission procedure set out in Section 8 below.

## 2. Object and scope of the procurement (Hankinnan kohde)

The procurement covers HVAC (LVI) maintenance, electrical maintenance, kitchen refrigeration equipment, and related technical upkeep of the city's school properties, as further specified in Liite A. The successful tenderer will act as the sole technical maintenance contractor for the properties covered by this agreement for the duration of the contract.

## 3. Eligibility requirements (Osallistumisedellytykset)

A tenderer must meet all of the following conditions. Failure to meet any one of them will result in exclusion from the procurement procedure.

3.1 The tenderer must have completed at least three (3) comparable reference projects within the last five years.

3.2 The tenderer's turnover in its most recent financial year must be at least EUR 5,000,000.

3.3 The tenderer's equity ratio, as shown in its most recent financial statements, must be at least 20%.

## 4. Technical requirements (Tekniset vaatimukset)

4.1 The tender shall describe the content and scheduling of routine maintenance visits at each school property.

4.2 The tender shall include a preventive maintenance plan for the systems covered by this contract.

4.3 The tender shall describe the availability of spare parts and the standard delivery time for parts not held in stock.

4.4 The tender shall describe the handover of the asset register and supporting documentation to the contracting authority at the start and end of the contract.

4.5 The tender shall describe the energy-efficiency measures the tenderer proposes to implement or monitor across the properties covered by this agreement.

4.6 The tender shall state the response time offered for fault reports and describe the standby (on-call) arrangement covering evenings, weekends and public holidays.

4.7 The tender shall describe the commissioning and testing procedure applied following any repair or installation work.

4.8 The tender shall describe the tenderer's arrangements for the periodic statutory inspection of electrical installations.

4.9 The tender shall provide a method statement for the installation work covered by this agreement.

## 5. Commercial terms (Kaupalliset ehdot)

5.1 The tender shall describe any discount structure offered against the overall volume of the framework agreement.

5.2 The tender shall state the tenderer's proposed payment terms.

5.3 The tender shall describe any bonus/malus arrangement proposed against the service levels in Section 4.

5.4 The tender shall state the tenderer's proposed limitation-of-liability clause.

## 6. Forms to be enclosed (Liitettavat lomakkeet)

6.1 The tender shall enclose curricula vitae of the key personnel proposed for delivery of the contract.

6.2 The tender shall include a certificate confirming payment of the tenderer's taxes.

## 7. Format requirements (Tarjouksen muotovaatimukset)

7.1 The minimum font size used throughout the tender document shall be 11pt.

7.2 All monetary figures in the tender shall be rounded to two decimal places.

7.3 The tender shall be marked with the time zone and exact time used for the submission deadline stated on its cover page.

## 8. Submission instructions

The tender, together with all appendices, must be submitted by email to tarjoukset@keski-vaara.example no later than 19.10.2026 at 12:00 (EET). Tenders received after this time will not be considered. Tenders shall be drafted in Finnish. Clarifying questions may be submitted in writing to the contact person named above no later than 28.09.2026; answers will be circulated to all parties who have requested the tender documents.

## 9. Evaluation criteria

Tenders that meet the eligibility requirements in Section 3 and the mandatory requirements elsewhere in this invitation will be evaluated on price (55%) and quality (45%). The quality score is based on the responses given under Section 4. The contracting authority reserves the right to reject all tenders without giving a reason.

## Liite A — Tekninen erittely (Technical specification)

This appendix sets out further technical detail supplementing Section 4. Tenderers must address each point below in their technical response.

A.1 The tender shall describe the qualification requirements the tenderer applies to personnel working on school premises.

A.2 The tender shall describe any training the tenderer proposes to provide to the contracting authority's own property staff on the systems maintained under this agreement.

A.3 The tender shall describe the method and frequency proposed for inspecting the condition of the building envelope (roof, facade, windows) at each school property.

A.4 The tender shall describe the maintenance regime proposed for refrigeration equipment (F-gas) in school kitchens.

## Liite B — Lomakkeet ja kaupalliset ehdot (Forms and commercial terms)

B.1 The tender shall confirm the tenderer's acceptance of the contractual penalty applied where the agreed service level under Section 4 is not met.

B.2 The tender shall include a certificate of the tenderer's pension insurance payments.

B.3 The tender shall be submitted using the Tender Form provided as Liite 1.
