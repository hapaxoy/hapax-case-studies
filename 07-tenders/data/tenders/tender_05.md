# Tender notice — Technical property maintenance framework agreement

**Contracting authority:** Keskijärven kaupunki, Tekninen toimiala
**Reference:** KESKIJKAU-2026-0143
**Procurement procedure:** Open procedure (avoin menettely)
**Notice published:** 14 September 2026
**Tender deadline:** 19 October 2026, 12:00 (EET)
**Planned contract start:** 1 January 2027
**Contract period:** 4 years, with two optional 1-year extensions at the contracting authority's discretion

## 1. Contracting authority and background

Keskijärven kaupunki (the "City") is procuring a framework agreement for technical
property maintenance services covering its municipal building stock, comprising
schools, day-care centres, health-service properties and administrative buildings
across the municipality. The current maintenance arrangement expires at the end of
2026 and the City intends to award a single framework agreement to one contractor
for the services described below.

This notice, together with its two annexes, forms the complete tender documentation.
Tenderers are expected to read both the body of this notice and the annexes before
submitting a tender; requirements are not repeated between the two.

## 2. Scope of the procurement

The framework agreement covers routine and corrective technical maintenance of
building services (heating, ventilation and air conditioning; electrical
installations; refrigeration and cooling plant; building automation) across
approximately forty municipal properties, together with associated reporting,
condition monitoring and preventive maintenance planning. Call-off orders will be
issued against the framework agreement by the City's property management unit as
needs arise; the framework agreement itself carries no minimum purchase commitment.

## 3. Eligibility requirements

Tenderers must satisfy the following requirements to be considered eligible. Proof
of compliance must be submitted with the tender as instructed in Section 6.

3.1 Tarjoajalla tulee olla voimassa oleva LUSA-patevyys kyseessa olevalle toimialalle.

3.2 Tarjoajalla tulee olla voimassa toiminnan vastuuvakuutus, jonka kattavuus on vahintaan 1 000 000 euroa.

3.3 Tarjoajan omavaraisuusasteen tulee olla vahintaan 20 %.

3.4 Tarjoajan liikevaihdon tulee olla vahintaan 3 000 000 euroa viimeisimmalla tilikaudella.

A tenderer that does not meet all four eligibility requirements will not have its
tender evaluated further.

## 8. Evaluation criteria and contract award

The framework agreement will be awarded to the economically most advantageous
tender on the following basis:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Price | 55% |
| Quality of the maintenance plan and reporting model | 30% |
| References and key personnel | 15% |

Price is evaluated on the unit rates submitted in accordance with Section 7 above.
Quality is evaluated on the technical response to Section 4. The City reserves the
right to request clarifications from tenderers during evaluation without altering
the substance of a tender.

## 9. Submission instructions

Tenders must be submitted in full accordance with the annexes to this notice by the
deadline given on the first page. Tenders received after the deadline will not be
opened. Questions concerning this notice may be submitted in writing up to ten (10)
days before the deadline; answers will be circulated to all tenderers who have
registered an interest. The City does not reimburse any costs incurred in preparing
a tender.
