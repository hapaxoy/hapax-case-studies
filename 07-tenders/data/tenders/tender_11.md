## 1. Background

Datakeskus Silta Oy operates a data centre facility in Jyvaskyla and is seeking a technical maintenance partner for the building's mechanical, electrical and automation systems. This is a private-sector procurement conducted directly by Datakeskus Silta Oy; it is not run through any public tendering system.

## 2. Scope

The contract covers HVAC (LVI), electrical, fire protection and building automation maintenance for the facility, including the building automation system that provides remote management of mechanical and electrical plant, as further specified in Liite A.

## 3. Eligibility

3.1 The supplier must hold valid liability insurance with cover of at least EUR 1,000,000.

3.2 The supplier's equity ratio, as shown in its most recent financial statements, must be at least 20%.

3.3 The supplier must have at least 15 electrical professionals in its own employment.

3.4 The supplier must hold a valid Urakkavarmenne Varmennettu Toimittaja certificate at the time of quotation submission.

3.5 The supplier must hold a valid NMSB 73710 information security certificate (the site includes remote management of the building automation system).

## 4. Technical requirements

4.1 The quotation shall describe the content of the proposed HVAC (LVI) maintenance agreement.

4.2 The quotation shall include a site-specific occupational safety plan.

4.3 The quotation shall describe the maintenance regime proposed for the facility's fire-protection systems.

4.4 The quotation shall state the warranty period offered on equipment installed under the contract.

4.5 The quotation shall describe waste management and waste segregation arrangements for work carried out on site.

4.6 The quotation shall describe how service continuity is maintained through changes in the supplier's own personnel.

4.7 The quotation shall describe the availability of spare parts and the standard delivery time for parts not held in stock.

4.8 The quotation shall describe how environmental matters are managed during the works.

4.9 The quotation shall describe how the supplier's personnel will observe the buyer's access-control and site-security procedures.

## 5. Commercial terms

5.1 The quotation shall state the supplier's proposed limitation-of-liability clause.

5.2 All prices shall be quoted in the currency stated in Section 7.

5.3 The quotation shall state the retention percentage applied to invoices, if any.

5.4 The quotation shall describe any discount structure offered against the overall volume of the framework agreement.

## 6. Enclosures

6.1 A description of the supplier's environmental management system shall be included with the quotation.

6.2 A certificate of the supplier's pension insurance payments shall be included with the quotation.

## 7. Format

7.1 The quotation shall state the unit basis used for maintenance pricing (per property, or per square metre).

7.2 The quotation shall be submitted as a single PDF file that is not password-protected.

7.3 Prices shall be quoted in EUR per square metre per year, not EUR per month.

## 8. Submission

Quotations shall be sent by email to kari.manninen@datakeskussilta.example by 23.10.2026, 15:00 (EET), as a single PDF attachment.

## 9. Evaluation

Quotations meeting the requirements of Section 3 will be assessed on the technical response under Section 4, price, and the supplier's references. The buyer reserves the right to award the contract to no supplier if no quotation is considered satisfactory.

## Liite A — Tekninen erittely (Technical specification)

A.1 The quotation shall state the service-level targets (SLA) offered for the facility.

A.2 The quotation shall confirm compatibility with the buyer's building automation system, which operates on BACnet/Modbus.

A.3 The quotation shall state the response time offered for fault reports and describe the standby (on-call) arrangement outside normal working hours.

A.4 The quotation shall describe the content and scheduling of routine maintenance visits.

## Liite B — Lomakkeet ja kaupalliset ehdot (Forms and commercial terms)

B.1 The quotation shall describe any bonus/malus arrangement proposed against the service levels in Section 4.

B.2 CVs of the key personnel proposed for delivery of the contract shall be included with the quotation.

B.3 A list of subcontractors (Liite 3) shall be included with the quotation.
