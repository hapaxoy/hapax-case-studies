# REQUEST FOR QUOTATION — Technical Maintenance Services

**Myllyranta Elintarvike Oy**
Reference: MRE-2026-02
Issued: 16 February 2026

## 1. Background

Myllyranta Elintarvike Oy operates food production and cold storage facilities in the Jyvaskyla region. The company is inviting quotations for technical maintenance services covering refrigeration, HVAC, electrical and fire safety systems.

## 2. Scope

The contract covers scheduled and corrective maintenance at the properties listed in Appendix 2, including refrigeration and air conditioning equipment subject to F-gas regulation.

## 3. Eligibility requirements

3.1 Where the work includes refrigeration or air conditioning equipment, the tenderer must hold a valid F-gas qualification (F-kaasuasetuksen mukainen patevyys).

3.2 The tenderer must hold a valid Urakkavarmenne Varmennettu Toimittaja certificate at the time the tender is submitted.

## 4. Technical requirements

4.1 Maintenance of fire protection systems.

4.2 Response times for corrective maintenance.

4.3 Response time for emergency situations and the associated on-call arrangement.

4.4 Environmental management arrangements during the course of the work.

4.5 Handover of the asset register and associated documentation.

## 5. Commercial terms

5.1 Cost breakdown requirement for the quoted price.

5.2 Payment terms.

## 6. Submission requirements

6.1 CVs of the key personnel proposed for the contract.

6.2 Prices shall be set out in EUR/m²/year rather than EUR/month.

6.3 The quotation must be submitted as a single PDF file, without password protection.

6.4 The minimum font size used throughout the quotation document shall be 11 pt.

Tenders must be submitted by e-mail to hankinta@myllyrantaelintarvike.example no later than **20 March 2026, 12:00 (Helsinki time)**.

## 7. Evaluation

Tenders will be evaluated on price (45%), technical approach (35%) and references (20%).
