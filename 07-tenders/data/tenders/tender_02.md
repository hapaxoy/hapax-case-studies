# INVITATION TO TENDER — Electrical and HVAC Maintenance Framework Agreement

**Vuorikylan kunta**
Tarjouspyynto / Procurement notice
Dnro: 58/02.08.01/2026
Issued: 2 April 2026

## 1. Background and contracting authority

Vuorikylan kunta is inviting tenders for a framework agreement covering electrical and HVAC maintenance of municipal buildings — schools, day-care centres and administrative premises — within the municipality. This procurement is conducted in accordance with the municipality's procurement guidelines; where the requirements of the Act on Public Procurement and Concession Contracts (hankintalaki) apply, this invitation is issued within that framework.

## 2. Scope of the procurement

The framework agreement covers scheduled and corrective maintenance of electrical and HVAC installations at the properties listed in Appendix 2, for the initial period set out in Section 8 of the draft agreement (issued to tenderers meeting the eligibility requirements below).

## 3. Eligibility requirements (mandatory)

A tenderer not meeting both of the following conditions will be excluded from further evaluation.

3.1 The tenderer must hold electrical contracting rights (sahkourakointioikeudet) of at least class S2.

3.2 The tenderer must have at least one reference project with a value of at least EUR 300,000.

## 4. Technical requirements

Tenderers must describe, for each of the following, how the requirement will be met:

4.1 Proposed service level targets (SLA).

4.2 Environmental management arrangements during the course of the work.

4.3 Quality control and testing procedures.

4.4 Content of the proposed HVAC system maintenance agreement.

## 5. Commercial terms

5.1 Limitation of liability clause proposed by the tenderer.

5.2 Price indexation clause.

## 6. Attachments and submission format

6.1 A tenderer intending to use subcontractors must complete and attach the Subcontractor List (Appendix 3).

6.2 The tender must be submitted as a single PDF file, without password protection.

6.3 Price must be quoted in EUR/m²/year, not EUR/month.

Tenders must be submitted via the municipality's electronic tender submission system by **15 May 2026, 12:00 (Helsinki time)**. Late tenders will not be opened.

## 7. Evaluation criteria

Tenders meeting the eligibility requirements in Section 3 will be evaluated on price (40%), technical quality (40%) and environmental management (20%). The contracting authority reserves the right to discontinue the procurement procedure at any stage.

## Annex A — Tekniset ja kaupalliset liitteet (Technical and Commercial Appendices)

A.1 Content and timing of the maintenance visit schedule.

A.2 Waste management and on-site sorting of waste arising from the works.

A.3 Description of the tenderer's environmental management system, to be attached to the tender.
