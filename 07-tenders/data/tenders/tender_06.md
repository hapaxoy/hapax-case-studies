# Request for quotation — Technical maintenance framework agreement

**Buyer:** Teollisuuspuisto Rautaportti Oy
**Reference:** RAUTAPORTTI-RFQ-2026-09
**Issued:** 8 September 2026
**Quotation deadline:** 6 October 2026, 15:00 (EET)
**Planned contract start:** 1 December 2026
**Contract period:** 3 years

## 1. Background

Teollisuuspuisto Rautaportti Oy owns and manages the building stock of an
industrial estate comprising production, warehouse and office buildings let to
multiple tenant companies. We are seeking a single contractor to provide
technical maintenance services across the estate under a framework agreement.
This is a request for quotation, not a public procurement notice; we run our own
supplier selection process and are not bound by public procurement rules, but we
expect quotations to address every point below in full.

## 2. Scope

The framework agreement covers electrical and building-services maintenance,
elevator servicing, fire-protection system servicing and related technical
maintenance across the estate's buildings, together with reporting to estate
management. Individual work orders will be issued against the framework
agreement as needs arise across the contract period.

## 3. Eligibility

The following must be satisfied for a quotation to be considered.

3.1 Tarjoajalla tulee olla voimassa oleva LUSA-patevyys kyseessa olevalle toimialalle.

3.2 Tarjoajalla tulee olla voimassa toiminnan vastuuvakuutus, jonka kattavuus on vahintaan 1 000 000 euroa.

3.3 Tarjoajalla tulee olla sahkourakointioikeudet (vahintaan luokka S2).

## 8. Evaluation

We will evaluate quotations on price (60%) and on the quality of the technical
response required under Section 4 (40%). We may contact tenderers for
clarification before making a decision. We do not commit to awarding the
framework agreement to the lowest-priced quotation and reserve the right to
cancel this process at any stage without giving reasons.

## 9. Submission

Quotations must be submitted in full accordance with the annexes to this
document by the deadline stated above. Late quotations will not be considered.
Questions should be directed to estate management in writing; we will circulate
answers to all tenderers who have submitted a question.
