# Request for quotation — Technical property maintenance services

**Buyer:** Kauppakeskus Solmu Oy
**Reference:** SOLMU-RFQ-2026-14
**Issued:** 10 September 2026
**Quotation deadline:** 30 September 2026, 16:00 (EET)
**Planned contract start:** 1 November 2026
**Contract period:** 3 years, with the option described in Section 5

## 1. Background

Kauppakeskus Solmu Oy operates a shopping centre and is seeking a single
contractor to provide technical property maintenance services for the centre's
building systems and common areas. This is a request for quotation; we run our
own supplier selection and are not bound by public procurement rules, but we
expect quotations to be complete against every point below.

## 2. Scope

The contract covers routine technical maintenance of building services,
elevators and fire-protection systems, together with fault response, across the
shopping centre. Maintenance visits and fault call-outs will be scheduled and
requested by centre management across the contract period.

## 3. Eligibility

The following must be satisfied for a quotation to be considered.

3.1 Tarjoajalla tulee olla voimassa toiminnan vastuuvakuutus, jonka kattavuus on vahintaan 1 000 000 euroa.

3.2 Tarjoajalla tulee olla sahkourakointioikeudet (vahintaan luokka S2).

3.3 Tarjoajalla tulee olla vahintaan kolme (3) vastaavaa referenssikohdetta viimeisen viiden vuoden ajalta.

3.4 Tarjoajan liikevaihdon tulee olla vahintaan 5 000 000 euroa viimeisimmalla tilikaudella.

## 8. Evaluation

We will evaluate quotations on price (55%), technical quality of the response
required under Section 4 (30%) and references (15%). We may contact tenderers
for clarification before making a decision and reserve the right to cancel this
process at any stage without giving reasons.

## 9. Submission

Quotations must be submitted in full accordance with the annexes to this
document by the deadline stated above. Late quotations will not be considered.
Questions should be directed to centre management in writing; we will circulate
answers to all tenderers who have submitted a question.
