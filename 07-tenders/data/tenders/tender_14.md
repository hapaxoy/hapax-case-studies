# Request for Quotation

**Buyer:** Konepaja Ryhtila Oy
**Subject:** Technical maintenance services — HVAC, electrical, and building systems for the Ryhtila production and office facility
**Reference:** KR-RFQ-2026-11
**Date of issue:** 15 September 2026

---

## 1. Background and scope

Konepaja Ryhtila Oy operates a metal-engineering production facility with an attached office wing outside Jyvaskyla. The facility's HVAC, electrical, building automation, and life-safety systems currently require an external maintenance contractor, and the company is inviting quotations for a maintenance agreement covering these systems for the production hall, machine shop, and office areas. This is a private-sector procurement and is not subject to public procurement rules; the process described below reflects the company's own purchasing practice.

Bidders should read this request together with its four annexes, which are issued alongside this document and form part of the request.

## 2. Eligibility requirements

2.1 The Bidder's turnover for the most recently closed financial year shall be at least EUR 3,000,000.

2.2 The Bidder shall employ at least 15 electrical professionals in its own workforce.

2.3 The Bidder shall have at least three (3) comparable reference projects within the last five years.

2.4 The Bidder shall hold a valid Urakkavarmenne Varmennettu Toimittaja certificate at the time the quotation is submitted.

2.5 The Bidder shall hold an F-gas regulation qualification where the work involves refrigeration or air-conditioning equipment.

2.6 The Bidder's turnover for the most recently closed financial year shall be at least EUR 5,000,000.

Bidders not meeting all of the above will not be considered further.

## 3. Technical requirements

3.1 Environmental management during works — describe how environmental impacts (waste, emissions, noise) will be managed during maintenance and repair work on site.

3.2 Content of the HVAC system maintenance agreement — describe the scope and frequency of scheduled HVAC maintenance proposed.

3.3 Inspection of the condition of the building envelope — describe how the condition of roofing, cladding, and building fabric will be assessed as part of the maintenance scope.

3.4 Quality control and testing procedures — describe internal quality control and testing procedures applied to completed work.

3.5 Warranty period for installed equipment — state the warranty period offered on equipment and materials installed.

3.6 Access control and security at the Buyer's premises — describe how site-access and security procedures will be observed by maintenance personnel working within an active production facility.

3.7 Periodic inspection of electrical installations — describe the programme for statutory periodic inspection of electrical installations.

3.8 Content and scheduling of maintenance visits — state the proposed content and frequency of scheduled maintenance visits.

3.9 Handover of the asset register and documentation — describe how the existing asset register and technical documentation will be received and maintained.

3.10 Commissioning and testing procedure — describe the commissioning and testing procedure following equipment replacement.

3.11 Method statement for installation works — provide a method statement for carrying out installation work without disrupting production.

3.12 Escalation procedure for unresolved faults — describe the escalation path for a fault not resolved within the agreed response time.

3.13 Fire protection system maintenance — describe the approach to scheduled testing and maintenance of fire protection systems.

3.14 Continuity of service through staff changes — describe how continuity will be maintained if key personnel leave or are reassigned.

3.15 Condition-monitoring data collection — describe how condition-monitoring data will be collected and reported to the Buyer.

3.16 Spare parts availability and delivery time — state typical lead times for common spare parts.

6.1 The quotation document shall not exceed 20 pages, excluding attachments.

6.2 Prices shall be quoted in EUR per square metre per year, not in EUR per month.

6.3 All prices shall be quoted excluding VAT, in euro.

6.4 Quotations shall note the time zone and closing time given for the submission deadline in Section 7.

6.5 The quotation shall be submitted as a single PDF file, without password protection.

6.6 Bidders should note that the closing time given in Section 7 is stated in Finnish time and refers to the time the quotation is recorded as received by the Buyer's mailbox, not the time it is sent; a quotation logged as received after 14:00:00 in that time zone, however briefly, will be treated as late regardless of the sender's own local time or system clock.

# Liite A — Tekninen erittely

This annex sets out the technical scope in more detail. The facility comprises the main production hall (approx. 6,200 m²), an attached machine shop bay, and a two-storey office wing (approx. 1,100 m²).

A.1 Regulatory compliance of lift maintenance — the office wing's passenger lift requires maintenance in compliance with applicable lift regulations; describe how this will be met and documented.

A.2 Reporting practice to the Buyer — describe how maintenance activity, findings, and outstanding items will be reported to the Buyer's facilities contact.

A.3 Qualification requirements for personnel on site — list the trade qualifications and site-induction requirements personnel will hold when working in the production areas.

A.4 Subcontracting management plan — describe how any subcontracted trades will be selected and supervised.

A.5 Preventive maintenance plan — submit a preventive maintenance plan covering the system categories described above.

A.6 Response time for fault situations and on-call arrangement — state the proposed response time by severity category and describe out-of-hours cover.

A.7 Response times for corrective maintenance — state proposed response times for corrective (non-emergency) maintenance requests, distinct from the fault-response times above.

General note: production-area access requires prior notice to the shift supervisor; maintenance work affecting production lines shall be scheduled outside core production hours wherever practicable.

# Liite D — Sopimusluonnos

This draft sets out the terms on which Konepaja Ryhtila Oy intends to contract with the successful bidder. It is issued for information alongside the request and will be finalised with the chosen bidder before signature.

**1. Parties.** The agreement will be between Konepaja Ryhtila Oy ("the Buyer") and the successful bidder ("the Contractor").

**2. Subject matter and term.** The Contractor will provide the maintenance services described in this request and its annexes from the contract start date stated in Section 7, for an initial period of two years.

**3. Price.** Pricing is as quoted by the Contractor and accepted by the Buyer, subject to the commercial terms in Liite B.

**4. Enclosures.** The Contractor's quotation, including all forms and attachments required by this request — the tender form (Liite 1), the reference form (Liite 2), and the subcontractor list (Liite 3) where applicable — is incorporated into and forms part of this agreement by reference.

**5. Personnel and access.** The Contractor shall ensure personnel meet the qualification and access requirements described in Liite A and observe the Buyer's site rules at all times.

**6. Liability.** Each party's liability is limited as set out in the Contractor's accepted commercial terms, save for liability that cannot be limited by law.

**7. Termination.** Either party may terminate for material breach not remedied within 30 days of written notice. The Buyer may terminate for convenience on three months' written notice.

**8. Governing law.** The agreement is governed by Finnish law.

**9. Signatures.** [To be completed on execution of the agreement.]
