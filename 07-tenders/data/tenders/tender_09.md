## 1. Contracting authority (Hankintayksikko)

Jarvimaan kunta is procuring, on behalf of its technical services department, a framework agreement for the technical maintenance of municipal properties. The procurement is carried out in accordance with the principles of national public procurement legislation and the contracting authority's own procurement instructions. This is not a request submitted through any national tendering portal; tenderers are asked to follow the submission procedure set out in Section 8 below.

## 2. Object and scope of the procurement (Hankinnan kohde)

The procurement covers the technical maintenance of the following municipal properties: Jarvimaan yhtenaiskoulu, paivakoti Tuulentupa, Jarvimaan terveysasema, and the municipal office building at Esimerkkikatu 8. The scope includes HVAC (LVI) maintenance, electrical maintenance, refrigeration and air-conditioning equipment, fire protection systems, lift maintenance, and periodic building-condition inspection, as further specified in Liite A. The successful tenderer will act as the sole technical maintenance contractor for the properties listed above for the duration of the agreement.

## 3. Eligibility requirements (Osallistumisedellytykset)

A tenderer must meet all of the following conditions. Failure to meet any one of them will result in exclusion from the procurement procedure.

3.1 The tenderer must hold a valid qualification under the F-Gas Regulation, where the works include refrigeration or air-conditioning equipment.

3.2 The tenderer must have completed at least one reference project with a contract value of no less than EUR 300,000.

3.3 The tenderer must have completed at least one reference project with a contract value of no less than EUR 500,000.

3.4 The tenderer's equity ratio, as shown in its most recent financial statements, must be at least 20%.

## 4. Technical requirements (Tekniset vaatimukset)

The tender must address each of the following points. Responses should be specific to the properties listed in Section 2.

4.1 The tender shall describe the content and scheduling of routine maintenance visits, including the annual visit calendar, the standard scope of tasks carried out at each visit, and the method used to confirm completion of each visit to the contracting authority.

4.2 The tender shall describe the response time offered for fault reports and the standby (on-call) arrangement covering evenings, weekends and public holidays.

4.3 The tender shall describe how the contractor's personnel will observe the contracting authority's access-control and site-security procedures while working on municipal premises, including school and day-care premises during operating hours.

4.4 The tender shall describe the contractor's arrangements for the periodic statutory inspection of electrical installations.

4.5 The tender shall describe how environmental matters — waste segregation, handling of hazardous substances, and spill prevention — are managed during the course of the works.

4.6 The tender shall include a site-specific occupational safety plan covering all properties listed in Section 2.

4.7 The tender shall describe the method and frequency proposed for inspecting the condition of the building envelope (roof, facade, windows) at each property.

4.8 The tender shall state the response times offered for corrective (reactive) maintenance work, broken down by fault category.

4.9 The tender shall describe the qualification requirements the tenderer applies to personnel working on site, including any occupational cards or trade certificates required of subcontracted staff.

## 5. Commercial terms (Kaupalliset ehdot)

5.1 The tender shall state the price index clause proposed for adjusting the contract price over the term of the agreement.

5.2 All prices in the tender shall be quoted in the currency stated in Section 7 of this invitation.

5.3 The tender shall confirm the tenderer's acceptance of the contractual penalty applied where the agreed service level under Section 4 is not met, as set out further in Liite B.

5.4 The tender shall state the tenderer's proposed invoicing frequency and invoicing format.

## 6. Forms to be enclosed (Liitettavat lomakkeet)

6.1 The tender shall enclose curricula vitae of the key personnel proposed for delivery of the contract.

6.2 The tender shall be submitted using the Tender Form provided as Liite 1.

## 7. Format requirements (Tarjouksen muotovaatimukset)

7.1 Prices shall be quoted in EUR per square metre per year (EUR/m²/vuosi); prices quoted on any other basis, including a monthly basis, will not be accepted for comparison.

7.2 The tender shall be submitted as a single PDF file. Password-protected files will not be opened and will be treated as not received.

7.3 All prices shall be quoted exclusive of value-added tax, in euros.

7.4 The minimum font size used throughout the tender document shall be 11pt.

## 8. Submission instructions

The tender, together with all appendices, must be submitted by email to tarjoukset@jarvimaankunta.example no later than 20.10.2026 at 12:00 (EET). Tenders received after this time will not be considered. Tenders shall be drafted in Finnish. Clarifying questions may be submitted in writing to the contact person named above no later than 29.09.2026; answers will be circulated to all parties who have requested the tender documents.

## 9. Evaluation criteria

Tenders that meet the eligibility requirements in Section 3 and the mandatory requirements elsewhere in this invitation will be evaluated on price (60%) and quality (40%). The quality score is based on the responses given under Section 4. The contracting authority reserves the right to reject all tenders without giving a reason.

## Liite A — Tekninen erittely (Technical specification)

This appendix sets out further technical detail supplementing Section 4. Tenderers must address each point below in their technical response.

A.1 The maintenance contract for the HVAC (LVI) system shall cover, at minimum, quarterly inspection of air-handling units, annual cleaning of ductwork in kitchen and food-preparation areas, and a documented log of measurements taken at each visit.

A.2 The tender shall describe the availability of spare parts for the installed building systems and state the standard delivery time for parts not held in stock.

A.3 The tender shall describe the maintenance regime proposed for fire-protection systems, including smoke detection, fire dampers, and emergency lighting, at each property.

A.4 The tender shall confirm that lift maintenance will be carried out in accordance with the applicable regulatory inspection regime and shall describe the tenderer's arrangements for statutory periodic lift inspections.

## Liite B — Lomakkeet ja kaupalliset ehdot (Forms and commercial terms)

This appendix sets out the retention terms and the forms required in support of the tender.

B.1 A retention of the amount stated in the draft contract will be withheld from each invoice and released on the terms set out in the draft contract enclosed with these tender documents.

B.2 The tender shall include a certificate of insurance confirming the cover held by the tenderer, consistent with the tenderer's own statements elsewhere in the tender.

B.3 The tender shall include the completed Reference Form provided as Liite 2, listing the reference projects relied upon under Section 3.

Tenderers should note that the contracting authority applies its standard enclosure practice for framework agreements of this type: in addition to the forms named above, and consistent with the contracting authority's usual requirements for service providers working on municipal premises, the tender must also include a certificate of the tenderer's pension insurance payments (elakevakuutusmaksut). No separate form is provided for this purpose; a certificate from the tenderer's pension insurance provider is sufficient.
