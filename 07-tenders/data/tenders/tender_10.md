## 1. Background

Varastopalvelu Kuormatie Oy operates three warehouse and logistics properties in the Jyvaskyla region and is seeking a single technical maintenance partner for these sites. This is a private-sector procurement; it is not run through any public tendering system, and quotations are evaluated at the buyer's discretion.

## 2. Scope

The contract covers HVAC (LVI), electrical, refrigeration, building automation and lift maintenance across the three sites, plus periodic energy and fault-response services as set out below and in Liite A.

## 3. Eligibility

3.1 The supplier must hold a valid LUSA qualification for the relevant field.

3.2 The supplier must hold valid liability insurance with cover of at least EUR 1,000,000.

3.3 The supplier must have at least 20 HVAC (LVI) professionals in its own employment.

3.4 The supplier's turnover in its most recent financial year must be at least EUR 5,000,000.

## 4. Technical requirements

4.1 The quotation shall include a site-specific occupational safety plan.

4.2 The quotation shall describe the condition-monitoring data the supplier collects during maintenance visits and how it is reported back to the buyer.

4.3 The quotation shall describe how environmental matters are managed during the works.

4.4 The quotation shall describe the supplier's quality-control and testing procedures.

4.5 The quotation shall include a preventive maintenance plan for the systems covered by this contract.

4.6 The quotation shall describe the content of the proposed HVAC (LVI) maintenance agreement.

4.7 The quotation shall describe the qualification requirements applied to personnel working on site.

4.8 The quotation shall confirm compatibility with the buyer's building automation system, which operates on BACnet/Modbus.

4.9 The quotation shall describe the content and scheduling of routine maintenance visits.

4.10 The quotation shall state the service-level targets (SLA) offered for each of the three sites.

## 5. Commercial terms

5.1 The quotation shall state the proposed contract period and any extension options offered.

5.2 The quotation shall state the supplier's proposed limitation-of-liability clause.

5.3 The quotation shall describe any bonus/malus arrangement proposed against the service levels in Section 4.

5.4 The quotation shall state the proposed pricing structure (fixed price / unit price / time-and-materials).

## 6. Enclosures

6.1 A list of subcontractors (Liite 3) shall be included with the quotation.

6.2 A completed Reference Form (Liite 2) shall be included with the quotation.

## 7. Format

7.1 The minimum font size used in the quotation shall be 11pt.

7.2 The quotation shall state the reference currency used for pricing under this framework agreement.

7.3 The quotation document shall not exceed 20 pages, excluding appendices.

7.4 The quotation shall state the unit basis used for maintenance pricing (per property, or per square metre).

7.5 Prices shall be quoted in EUR per square metre per year, not EUR per month.

## 8. Submission

Quotations shall be sent by email to tuula.lehto@kuormatie.example by 16.10.2026, 14:00 (EET), as a single PDF attachment. Quotations submitted after this time may be disregarded at the buyer's discretion.

## 9. Evaluation

Quotations will be assessed on price, the technical response under Section 4, and the supplier's references. The buyer is not obliged to accept the lowest-priced quotation and may negotiate further with one or more suppliers before award.

## Liite A — Tekninen erittely (Technical specification)

A.1 The supplier's maintenance regime shall confirm that lift maintenance is carried out in accordance with the applicable regulatory inspection requirements.

A.2 The quotation shall describe the energy-efficiency measures the supplier proposes to implement or monitor at the three sites.

A.3 The quotation shall state the response time offered for fault reports and describe the standby (on-call) arrangement outside normal working hours.

A.4 The quotation shall describe the escalation procedure applied to faults that remain unresolved after the initial response.

B.1 The quotation shall describe how subcontractor costs are made transparent to the buyer, including any mark-up applied.

B.2 A current trade register extract for the supplier shall be included with the quotation.

B.3 The quotation shall be submitted using the Tender Form provided as Liite 1.
