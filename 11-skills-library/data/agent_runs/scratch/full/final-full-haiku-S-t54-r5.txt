Based on my investigation of the Tuomimetsä warehouse, I've found the whole company's net sales for September 2026 (last month).

The authoritative source is the `rpt_daily_store_sales` table, which is maintained by Data &amp; Analytics. This table aggregates daily sales across all stores with returns already factored in.

**September 2026 Total Company Net Sales: 1,179,035.98 EUR**

The query used is:
```sql
SELECT SUM(net_sales) AS net_sales
FROM rpt_daily_store_sales
WHERE business_date &gt;= '2026-09-01' AND business_date &lt; '2026-10-01'
```
