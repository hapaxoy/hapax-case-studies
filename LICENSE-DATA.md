## Exception: 04-contracts derived answer-key files

`04-contracts/data/answer_key/` and `04-contracts/data/manifest.json` are
Hapax adaptations of the CUAD v1 dataset's `master_clauses.csv`. CUAD is
© The Atticus Project and is also released under CC BY 4.0, but its own
attribution and citation requirements govern those specific files — see
`04-contracts/data/CUAD_LICENCE_ATTRIBUTION.md`, which must be read and
carried forward alongside any redistribution of that folder.

The underlying CUAD contract archive itself is not redistributed in this
repository. `04-contracts/data/contracts/` contains the 150-contract
sub-corpus this case selected from it (itself CC BY 4.0, per the same
licence); the full archive's source URL and checksum are recorded in
`04-contracts/data/manifest.json`.
